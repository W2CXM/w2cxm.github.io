Repeaters

The Cornell Amateur Radio Club maintains two open-access repeaters on the Cornell University campus in Ithaca, Tompkins County, New York. Characteristics of the repeaters are presented below. These complement the existing breadth of repeaters available in the county as maintained by the Tompkins County Amateur Radio Association (TCARA), for which more information can be found here. Answers to any questions you might have about the repeaters can be addressed by emailing the club officers at w2cxm@cornell.edu.

61 Repeater (VHF)

  • Operating Frequency: 146.610 MHz
  • Offset: 600 kHz (-)
  • CTCSS Tone: 103.5 Hz
  • Building: Bradfield Hall, Cornell University
  • Location: Ithaca, Tompkins County, New York
  • Access: Open Repeater

050 Repeater (UHF)

  • Operating Frequency: 444.050 MHz
  • Offset: 5 MHz (+)
  • CTCSS Tone: 103.5 Hz
  • Building: Rhodes Hall, Cornell University
  • Location: Ithaca, Tompkins County, New York
  • Access: Open Repeater
